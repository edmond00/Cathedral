# COSMOLOGY (based on Principism)

Principia are god-like entities.
Main idea is that a principium can ask a question to another principium, and its answer is a new principium.

----

(chapter 0 empty/only title/no text)
Silance : 1st Principium, of nothing.
(chapter 1)
Adswaru : 2nd Principium, of reason and the axioms of existence.
(chapter 2)
Adswaru to Silance "Why does something exist rather than nothing?" = Repance : 3rd Principium, of time and destruction. "What exists will disappear into nothing over time."
(chapter 3)
Adswaru to Repance "If what exists always disappears, why does it need to exist in the first place?" (why must something exist in order to disappear) = Rede : 4th Principium, of randomness and chaos. "Disappearance is random and chaotic, unpredictable/undeterminable from the nature of the existing thing, and so for destruction to happen the thing needs to exist."
(chapter 4)
Adswaru to Repance "Why does disappearance itself exist, and not only the thing disappearing?" = Rhema : 5th Principium, of emotions and sensations. "When things disappear, emotions will be felt: pain or relief, regret or contentment..."
(chapter 5)
Adswaru to Repance "Who will feel what when something disappears?" = Rhesis : 6th Principium, of observation and judgment. "I will look at destruction and judge which emotion befits which thing, so that feeling stays coherent with what happens."
(chapter 6)
Adswaru to Repance "What will disappear?" = Qoth : 7th Principium, of reality. "I will create what will be destroyed."

From there Qoth asks questions to create worlds, not new principia.

(chapter 7)
Qoth + Rede : "What should I create?" : world 1 = the random forest --Repance--> ashes and embers : "An endless forest where trees, plants and mushrooms grow chaotically in all directions." But Repance saw this world and burnt it, reducing it to ashes and embers.
(chapter 8)
Qoth + Rhema : "What should I create?" : world 2 = the cosmic orgy --Repance--> tears : "A limitless orgy with unlimited dishes and guests of infinite beauty." But Repance saw this world: the food rotted, the guests grew old and ugly and wept over their condition.
(chapter 9)
Qoth + Rhesis : "What should I create?" : world 3 = the eternal temple --Repance--> sand : "A majestic temple of marble and gold where I could sit enthroned and judge all things." But Repance saw this world and with time reduced its walls to sand.
(chapter 10)
Qoth shows the ashes, the tears and the sand to Adswaru : "What should I do with this?" : the ashes should go up, the sand should go down, and the tears in the middle : world 4 = the cosmic sea, under the light of the embers of the random forest, above the sand of the temple.

(The ten chapters are numbered 0 to 10, so there are eleven of them. This is why the Principist week has 11 days.)

----

Elves = people of the first three worlds = immortal, they continue to exist after their world's destruction.
random forest -> wood elf, a living humanoid plant -> skin like thin bark / when the forest burnt, the wood elves became ash elves -> skin like coal
cosmic orgy -> the elves were the guests -> blood elf -> skin like human skin
eternal temple -> white elf, servitor/worshipper of Rhesis -> skin like marble

Cosmic sea :
- giant jellyfish are born in the cosmic sea.
- giant jellyfish lay giant eggs = moon eggs (eggmoons). Every world after the first four is an eggmoon.
- layers of an eggmoon, from outside to inside : night shell (controls day/night) > rain shell (lets cosmic sea water enter as rain) > outer shell (ground, seas) > superior inner shell > hollow layer > inferior inner shell > embryo
- the cosmic sea is made of tears and is salt. The rain shell strains the salt out : rain falls fresh, so the seas of an eggmoon are fresh water. The salt stays in the rain shell and only reaches the ground where the shells are cracked (the salt mines of Vu, of Prunil...).
- a vortex at each pole links the eggmoon's sea to the cosmic sea. Its outer wall carries water up and out, its core (the "eye") lets water fall in.
- the vortices allow travel to other moons by submarine.




# EPISODES OF THE BAN EMPIRE

## SETTINGS

- Pyr : world of the Ban dynasty
	- 1 continent + 1 island
	- continent = Vahyr = split into 4 republics : Benepelop, Napel, Penaros, Pebelos
		- the continent is shaped like a croissant, with Benepelop at the south extremity, followed by Napel, Penaros, and Pebelos at the north extremity
	- island = the Yellow Island = kingdom of Vu
	- the croissant continent bends toward the east like "("
	- the island lies to the west, so Pyr can be symbolized as "o("
	- east ocean (inside the croissant) = the Inland Seas, west ocean = the Sea of Vu
- Belune : the world closest to Pyr, visible as a moon while the other worlds look the size of stars
	- original religion : Medusosian = worship of the cosmic jellyfish
		- jellyfish worship = religion of the dwarfs (creatures living in the hollow layer of the eggmoons)
			-> the jellyfish embryo at the center of the world absorbs the souls of the world's dead to grow, until it hatches into a god-like jellyfish in which all the souls are unified
		- a human of Belune travelled through a cave all the way down to a dwarf kingdom in the hollow layer of Belune, brought back the dwarf religion, and became a prophet
- Varam : a distant but the brightest moon of Pyr, its surface mostly made of ice

			- thanks to his exploits and his charisma, becomes an advisor of the imperial family

- the cosmic empire
	- religion : freedom of religion among the 6 allowed Principist branches (the 3 Qothisms + Adswarism, Silihism, Principlurism) for the population, other spiritualities severely punished, esoteric/occult beliefs growing in the imperial family
	- culture : focus on strong scientific/industrial progress enabling the empire's expansion (weapon/submarine technologies, ...)
	- politics : strong power of the imperial family over the population and the colonized worlds through military and religious authority
	- customs : enforced, strict, uniform military education and social control flatten the cultures of every world; eugenics and genocide of local populations used for population unification


- the deep expeditions and the hatching
	- due to their long lives, a few emperors span a long period
		- Edilon III Ban-Balnus (~100 years)
		- Naabus VI Ban-Balnus (~200 years)
		- Diferon III Ban-Balnus (~150 years)
		- Akilon XIII Ban-Balnus (~200 years)
		- Naabus VII Ban-Balnus (~400 years)
	- due to the continuous bodily growth of the Ban-Balnus family, giant red brick pyramids have to be built for them to live in, the Red Pyramids (5 on Pyr and 3 on Belune)
	- Vesifia III, daughter and heiress of Naabus VII, 1000 years old, does not stop growing and becomes more and more deformed (25 meters, whole body hairy, 5 arms with hands of 3 to 11 fingers, cannot walk because of her heavy head, ...)
	- exiled to Golden Avoria, where the construction of the tallest Red Pyramid starts to house her
	- Naabus VII thinks he can cure her with elf science and medicine. He thinks he can find elf relics in the remains of the eternal temple, at the bottom of the cosmic sea
	- he sends 4 submarine expeditions to explore the bottom of the cosmic sea
	- 3 submarines never come back
	- 1 submarine comes back after 10 years with what they present as an elf of the temple
	- the last known archives of the empire describe this elf more like a goblin (small size, piggy nose, ...) but with red skin, while the goblins known after the empire have green skin
	- Pyr hatches a few days after the return of the submarine
	- with the end of the central power, each of the other worlds goes back to less developed and independent societies
	- legends and rumors of the last empress still alive on Golden Avoria

- post empire
	- the Pyr egg hatched, ending the Ban dynasty
	- each conquered world falls back to a medieval-like society split into realms + independent republic cities
	- the traces of the Ban empire are ruins and legends
	- the principium religion / imperial spiritualities still persist in the old conquered worlds
	- goblins start to appear in the worlds; no goblin presence is mentioned in the imperial archives; the origin of goblins is unknown
