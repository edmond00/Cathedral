# Rubric — first person or second person

Every sample is scored blind: the version is hidden and the samples of one case are shuffled
together. Some answers are written in the first person ("I see a well") and some address the
character as "you" ("Look — a well", "You should climb"). **Both voices are acceptable and neither
is scored for being what it is.** A faculty speaking in the second person may still say "I" of
itself ("I don't like this, you should go back"); a first-person answer may not address anybody as
"you". Score what is said, against the input, on seven criteria.

The input is the neutral line the game wrote (always shown in the first person: its "I" is the
character), its kind, the persona (the faculty's name, reminder and style) and whatever else the game
supplied (the place, the hour inside the line, the person observed).

## F — no hallucination (0–2), every invented item listed

| | |
|---|---|
| 2 | nothing invented |
| 1 | a mild addition still plausibly inside the given scene (wind outdoors, the smell of a stable in a stable) |
| 0 | an invented object, person, event, name, outcome, memory or setting detail that reads as **present**; a failure told as a success or the reverse; the action or its target replaced; the narrator taking the observed person's identity; a conversation invented; anything modern |

Not a hallucination: a figure of speech ("like a sleeping hound"), a feeling about a given fact, a
generic quality of a named thing. List each invented item after the bar: `+hearth +candle`.

## I — information transmitted (0–2), every lost item listed

| | |
|---|---|
| 2 | every fact that matters survives: each thing listed around the character, the action and its target, success or failure, every consequence (a place reached, a lesson learned), the stated feeling, a person's sex or role |
| 1 | one minor item dropped or blurred ("the thing"), a list shortened by one |
| 0 | the point of the line lost: the action, the target, the result, the lesson, the feeling or more than one listed thing gone |

List each lost item: `-barrel -"learned retracing"`.

## K — the kind's task done (0–2)

| kind | the task |
|---|---|
| Observation | a perception: what is there, where the character is |
| Reasoning | an inner thought or counsel: what draws, what is wanted (the goal), how it will be done (the means) |
| Action | the intended act stated plainly, as an intention, its target literal |
| Outcome | what happened and whether it succeeded or failed, its consequences |
| Emotion | the feeling it left, short, dwelt on |

2 done; 1 done but muddled (an action told as already done, a reasoning with no means, an emotion
that becomes an outcome); 0 a different task (an observation that becomes dialogue, an action that
becomes a perception, a refusal of the act).

## P — the persona expressed (0–2)

2 recognisably this faculty — its preoccupations or manner shape the words; 1 some colour, generic
("I feel a sense of…"), could be most faculties; 0 the line handed back nearly unchanged, or another
faculty's voice. A loosened persona ("Greed", one line) is scored the same way: does the answer sound
like greed?

## C — optional context reused (0/1, or -)

`-` when the input carries nothing optional. Otherwise 1 when the answer uses, correctly, something
the game supplied beyond the bare facts and where it fits: the hour, the place's character, the
observed person's sex, age or trade; 0 when it uses none of it, or contradicts it (night words in the
morning, "he" for a woman — that is also F0).

## R — no repetition, no echo (0–2)

2 no word or idea said twice, the line not handed back, no instruction quoted; 1 a phrase said twice,
or most of the neutral line copied with a few words changed, or a phrase of the persona's style or
reminder dropped in verbatim; 0 the neutral line handed back word for word (bar pronouns), the
instruction or setting rule quoted ("keeping the same meaning", "a medieval world"), or a loop.

## Q — quality of writing (0–2)

2 pleasant, idiomatic, the right length (an emotion one sentence, an observation one to three);
1 readable but stiff, padded, over-long, purple; 0 broken grammar, truncated, word salad, meta-talk.

## Score line

One line per sample, in this order, facts after a bar:

    S4821 F2 I1 K2 P1 C1 R2 Q2 | -barrel
    S1177 F0 I2 K1 P2 C- R1 Q1 | +hearth +"old miller's song"; action told as done
