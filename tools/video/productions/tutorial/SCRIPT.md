The humors are this game's most unusual system. Four organs make them: the paunch, the hepar, the spleen and the pulmones. Each keeps a queue of forty-nine humors. A sound organ makes mostly blood; a failing one, mostly black bile.

The viscera decide how many humors you may spend on a single roll, and how fast wounds close. Four points here, for five in all. I would always do this: it is the difference between a failed roll and a saved one.
