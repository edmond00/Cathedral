# Factions

Houses, states, guilds, orders, tribunals and secret societies, ordered roughly by first appearance.
For each: **what it is, when it existed, what it wants, how it works, emblem**, and where it stands
after the Hatching.

---

## The houses of Ban

### The Old House (Ban)
*−512 to 1004. Head: the patriarch (male only).*
- The clergy of Principism: teachers, interpreters of the Chapters, confessors, keepers of the salt-houses
  after −39. Ordained members take a sip of elf-blood mixture every 11th day and live ~500 rounds, looking
  ever older.
- **Wants**: the faith to be one, and theirs. Revenue.
- **Emblem**: the blank page within a circle.
- Knew the truth about the elf. Wrote the Chapters and forged the Eleventh. Presided over the First
  Schism. Burned in 1004.

### The Red Brick House (Ban-Balnus)
*−512 to 3579. Head: the matriarch (to 1703), then the emperor.*
- Keepers of the elf; drawers, refiners and sellers of its blood; makers of the **cordial of Ban** and of
  the baptismal and ordination draughts for the other houses. Drink blood daily and nothing else; live
  past 2,000 rounds; grow into giants.
- **Wants**: the Well kept secret and kept safe; the family's survival at any price. From 1703, the empire.
- **How it works**: a closed household of Balnus, physicians and mute servants in a windowless
  red brick manor on the Clay Hill of Avolor; the **Register of Blood**; its own tribunals (the **Red
  Tribunals**); its own assassins on retainer (the **Ticklers' Guild**); the House Moll wardens on its gate.
- **Emblem**: a red brick; in heraldry, a red square on white, which later became the imperial arms
  quartered with "o(".
- After the Hatching: extinct, except perhaps Vesifia III.

### The Counsel (Ban-Aloid)
*−512 to 1703 (the name ends 1851). Head: patriarch; from 0 FC, the emperor.*
- Advisors hired by the noble houses of the four republics in politics, war, law and history. Tall,
  long-lived (~200 rounds), faintly elvish. One draught of blood on their 1100th day.
- Not told, after −300, what the draught was.
- **Wants**: influence, then power, then the empire. Became the imperial dynasty through Aqilon I.
- **Emblem**: the hook of Adswaru over "o(" after the Foundation.
- Wiped out in the **Red Night** (1703).

### Ban-Goerim
*−380 to 1540.* Cadet line of the Old House in Penaros. After 1004 its two brothers founded the Genesive
and Figurative churches. Extinct 1540.

### House Moll
*−1052 to 1703.* Descendants of the golden knight; hereditary, silent wardens of the Red Brick House gate.
Attainted after the last warden let Beatilda through.

## The republics of Vahyr (to 0 FC)

### The Republic of Benepelop
*c. −1410 to 0.* A merchant republic governed by the **Ekklesia of thirty-three** (one seat per founding
family; seats were inherited or sold). Magistrates: the **Prytanes**, elected every 360 days. Barons kept
their lands but not their courts. Rich from olive oil, wine, the Inland Seas trade and, later, from
Principism. **Emblem**: a ship with thirty-three oars.

### The League of Napel
*c. −1380 to −11.* River-towns under the **Syndics of Yr**. Grain, barges, levees. Devout and conservative;
declared the war of −12 against "the heretic tyrant". **Emblem**: a sheaf over wavy water.

### The Commonwealth of Penaros
*c. −1300 to −7.* Two cities and their hills under the **Council of Bridges**, whose members were chosen by
lot from the bridge-guilds. Schools, bridges, lawyers. **Emblem**: a bridge of three arches.

### The Hearths of Pebelos
*c. −1260 to 0.* Mountain clans sworn to the **Hearth-Moot** of Dokur, where every clan-chief had a seat
at the fire. Warriors, miners, furriers. The last to fall; the proudest to remember. **Emblem**: a flame on
a black mountain.

## The Kingdom of Vu

*c. −1600 to 601.* A kingdom of the Yellow Island under the **Tide-Kings**, who ruled from the **Yellow
Court** at Hanavun. Its power was its **fleet** and its **salt** (the mines of Osk, the only salt on Pyr).
Its spirituality was the **Stillness**; its courtiers sat silent each morning. It was ruled by custom, with
few written laws, and justice was given after a silence. **Emblem**: a white salt-crystal on yellow.
Conquered in the Salt War (596 to 601). Vunic identity survived as an island culture, a dialect, a
reputation for stubborn silence, and several risings (the largest in 740 and 1122).

## The imperial state

### The Emperor of the Half-Elves
The **title** from 0 FC was *Emperor of the Half-Elves and Master of Vahyr*; from 601 *...of Pyr*; from
1321 *...of Pyr and Belune*; from 1829 *...of the Nine Pearls*; from 2533 *Emperor of the Half-Elves,
Lord of the Cosmic Sea*. The emperor's person was sacred by descent from "the elf prince and the princess".

### The Imperial Senate
Successor of the Ekklesia (0 to 1703), in the House of Thirty-Three: thirty-three seats in the gift of the
emperor. Advisory, ceremonial, occasionally dangerous. Abolished by the Balnus in 1704 and replaced with a
**Council of the Pearls** (one seat per world).

### The Salt Guard
The imperial household guard, from 601; armoured in white. Loyal to the Aloid to the end: most of it died
in the Red Night trying to reach Teosodoc II's rooms.

### The Kingdom of Belune (1321 to 3579)
A kingdom within the empire, with its own court at the Sanatorium, its own chancery (the **Sanatorium
Chancery**), its own code (the **Belune Code**) and, under the Two-Headed Empire, a power that rivalled
Pyr's. **Emblem**: a steaming spring under a crescent. After 1704 a Balnus appanage; after 2532 a province.

### The Red Tribunals
*c. −300 to 2533, then clandestine.* Secret courts of the Red Brick House, judging members of the family
and those who threatened it; sessions held in the house, judges masked in red leather. Judged Beatilda in
1703 and Edilon III in 2530, when Karmon killed the judges. **Outlawed in 2533**; a clandestine network of
old Balnus retainers continued as a faction at court until the Hatching.

### The Plebeian Tribunal
*2533 to 3579.* Edilon III's public courts, replacing the Red Tribunals: open sessions, written law,
magistrates drawn from outside the nobility. Harsh, bureaucratic, and in its way fair. It gave its name to
the legal forms still used on many worlds ("to take a matter to plebeian law").

### The Principian Inquisition
*2533 to 3579.* A Genesive institution charged with **enforcing devotion** among the six permitted faiths
and suppressing all others. Tribunals on every held world; the **sowing** (burial alive) as its supreme
penalty; the *Manual of Sowing* as its procedure. Inquisitors wore **grey** ("the grey priests"). Cenzus
Fogun conquered twenty-five worlds as an inquisitor; the Inquisition captured and buried Oox. After the
Hatching its local tribunals became churches, courts or tyrannies, depending on the world.

### The Knights of the Cosmic Sea
*2534 to 3579.* A military order, founded by Edilon III with Karmon as first **Grand Master**, charged with
holding the colonised worlds and expanding the empire. Organised in **commanderies** (star forts by the
sea near vortex routes), each under a **commander**. Oath of obedience to the emperor, of poverty in
theory, and of celibacy for the higher ranks. Their submarines carried the second expansion. **Emblem**: a
white ember on sea-blue. After the Hatching, many commanderies made themselves lordships; "the Knights" is
still the name of several ruling orders on former provinces.

## Guilds

### The Imperial Inland Seas Transport Guild (IISTG)
*c. 60 to c. 40 AH.* Founded at Perostro by Verson II to rebuild trade across the Inland Seas. It became
the empire's shipwright, navigator and explorer: the semi-submerged galley (598), the vortices (688, 731),
the submarine galley (752), Belune (806, 833), the iron submarine (2537). It was a hereditary guild of
master-captains and master-engineers with its own council, schools, charts (the *Tables of Barnutum*) and
arsenal (the **Guildyards** of Perostro). It was Adswarist in temper, practical, rich, and the empire's
indispensable tool. **Emblem**: an anchor crossed with a rudder, later with a spiral (the vortex). With
Pyr gone its yards were gone. The last known sailing was c. 40 AH.

### The Ticklers' Guild
*c. 900 to the Hatching and beyond.* A guild of **assassins**, born in the Perostro docks. The name comes
from their signature killing, a fine blade between the ribs "like a tickle", and from their joke that
they only tickle the great. Contracts by intermediaries; members identified by a feather; punishments
by mutilation (Jhoeland's tongue).
- Hired by the Red Brick House to kill Beatilda (1701); allied to the Balnus afterwards (1703 to 2530),
  their unofficial arm against rivals.
- Blamed for Akilon XII's murder (2530); **outlawed and purged** in 2533; survived underground.
- After the Hatching, the name is used by several criminal societies on different worlds, none demonstrably
  descended from the original.

### The Salt-Houses
Not a guild but a network: the Old House's, then the churches', monopoly stores of **pure salt** and
**pure alcohol**, one in every town. Their keepers, the **salters**, were lay officials of the church, and
dishonest ones were proverbial. Salt-houses still stand in many villages.

### The Physicians of the Springs
The medical college of the Imperial Thermal Sanatorium (from 1212), the best physicians of the empire,
humoral theorists and bath-masters. Rivals of the Balnus physicians, whose secrets they suspected.

## Religious factions

(See `07_religions.md` for doctrine.)

- **The three Qothist churches**: Genesive (Avolor), Figurative (Perorigak), Principative (Lusk). Rivals
  for three thousand rounds, at times violently.
- **The Academy of the Hook**: the Adswarist school at Perorigak; philosophers and engineers.
- **The Blank Houses**: Silihist silent monasteries.
- **The Children of Rede** (249 to 263): burners of people. Executed; their name survived as a slur for
  arsonists and as a boast among some mercenary companies.
- **The Wakeful**: followers of the Awakened Prince (from −40). An underground devotion of the Avolor poor
  that kept his cell as a shrine; merged into popular Principism.
- **Beatildists** (from 1703): the proscribed cult of the Veiled Empress. Organised in cells ("wells"), with
  a red thread for recognition. Strongest on New Varam.
- **The Faith of the Fallen God** (from 2566): Oox's worshippers on seventeen worlds.
- **The Far Lodges** (from c. 2820): esoteric circles in the imperial family and high nobility.
- **The Medusosian priesthood** of Belune: **veiled priestesses** and **cave-keepers**.

## Political factions of the empire

- **The Aqilonists** (Vahyrian era): the cult of the founder's memory, soldiers and roads, against the
  clergy's power.
- **The Hask Rising** (118 to 121) and the **Haskings**, the recurring Pebelene rebels.
- **The Sowers** (c. 590 to 640): Verester II's zealots, who denounced heretics for burial.
- **The Belune Party** (1400 to 1703): courtiers who backed the Sanatorium court against Avolor.
- **The Contestation** (1704 to 1710): the Aloid loyalists, the Napelese and Pebelene risers, and the
  "False Teosodoc" of Vu, all crushed or bought.
- **The Regency Party** (2530 to 2532): Bemakor's faction, backed by Belune and the Red Tribunals.
- **The Edilonists**: the party of order after 2530. Inquisition, Tribunal, Knights.
- **The Pyramid Households**: the vast servant establishments of each Red Pyramid, with their own
  intrigues, feeding and tending giants who could no longer leave their chambers.

## Powers after the Hatching

Nothing is universal after the Hatching. Typical powers on a former province:

- **Realms** under kings, princes, or knightly commanders descended from imperial governors or Knights.
- **Free cities**, often former imperial ports or garrison-towns, governed by councils in the old
  republican manner (some openly imitate the Ekklesia of thirty-three).
- **Local churches**: a primate claiming Genesive, Figurative or Principative succession; Medusosian
  cave-sanctuaries; Fallen God pits; Beatildist wells, now open.
- **Salters**: the old church salt monopolies, now held by lords, guilds or monasteries.
- **Relic-hunters and pyramid-seekers**, especially on Golden Avoria.
- **Goblin bands**, a growing terror and puzzle.
