# Lineages

## The Register of Blood, and how the Ban number their names

Every Ban is entered in the **Register of Blood**, kept at the Red Brick House from −505 FC. The
Register records birth, parents, the date of baptism or ordination, the blood taken, and death.

**Numbering rule.** A name borne by one Ban carries no numeral. When a second Ban is given the
same name, the second is entered as **II**, and the first becomes **I** in retrospect. Numbers
therefore count **every Ban ever registered under that name, in order of birth, in every house**.
They are *not* regnal numbers: Verson II is "II" because his uncle, who never reigned, was the first
Verson. Two brothers can both be Poeloc (II and III).

The Register counts a name **as written**. The Ban-Balnus spelled their sons' name **Akilon**, in
honour of the conqueror **Aqilon** but in their own hand, so the Akilons (I to XIII, all Balnus)
and the Aqilons (I to V, all Aloid) are counted separately. When the Balnus took the throne they
chose one Aloid imperial name on purpose: **Edilon III** follows two Aloid emperors, Edilon I and II.

Every numbered name in these files is complete:

| name | bearers | house |
|---|---|---|
| Aqilon | I to V | Aloid |
| Akilon | I to XIII | Balnus |
| Verson | I to IV | Aloid |
| Poeloc | I to IV | Aloid |
| Teosodoc | I, II | Aloid |
| Verester | I, II | Aloid |
| Polop | I, II | Aloid |
| Ossiane | I, II | Aloid |
| Edilon | I, II (Aloid), III (Balnus) | Aloid, Balnus |
| Naabus | I to VII | Balnus |
| Diferon | I to III | Balnus |

Consorts outside the family are rare and noted. Most Ban married a sibling or a first cousin.

Notation: `name (born/died)`, dates in FC; `×` = union; `†` = violent death; `[P]` patriarch,
`[M]` matriarch, `[E]` emperor or empress regnant, `[K]` king or queen of Belune.

## 6. Other lineages

Simple lineages for important non-Ban figures.

### The Oskavun twins
- Father **Ravosh Oskavun**, net-mender of Tavunesh; mother **Hanu**, a salt-carrier's daughter from Osk.
- **Hanuvesh** and **Hanuvela**, twins (b. c. −1235). No other children survived. Neither married.

### House Moll, wardens of the Well's door
- **Ser Gauderic Moll** (−1078/−980), the golden knight → **Aimeric Moll** → ... a hereditary line of
  wardens, sworn to silence, holding the Red Brick House gatehouse for 2,700 rounds.
- **Hamelin Moll** (1650/1703), last warden. **[hidden]** He let Beatilda and Jhoeland through the gate
  out of love for the princess. Executed in 1703; House Moll attainted and extinguished.

### The Tide-Kings of Vu (last generations)
- **Kesunavosh** (b. 500; king 540 to 571) × Queen Aramavi
  - **Jevunavosh** (548/601), last king, × **Ilavena** of Osk
    - **Tavosh** (575/597), crown prince, drowned at Cape Lemavosh
    - **Imaravun** (580/660), taken hostage to the Salt Palace; became a Qothist nun known as **Sister
      Salt**, and wrote the *Litany of the Island*
- The line is extinct. Several post-imperial Vunic families claim descent from Imaravun, and so, it is
  said, did at least one Aloid emperor's mistress.

### The Oltenne (the Awakened Prince)
- **Amalric Oltenne**, last prince of Benepelop (deposed c. −1410); his descendants fell into trade.
- **Tobre Oltenne**, ropemaker of the Ropewalk × **Nell**, a washerwoman
  - **Cassimir** (−75/−31), the Awakened Prince. No children. A brother, **Pier**, was killed in the Quelling.

### House Hask (Vhomanor)
- **Hask the Elder** (−90/−20), clan-chief of the Sorvan hearths
  - **Vhomanor Hask** (−44/−1) × **Adelhaid** of Dokur
    - **Vhomer Hask** (−20/121), raised in Dokur; led the **Hask Rising** (118 to 121); executed.
      His children were dispersed; "Hasking" became the Pebelene word for a rebel.

### Grizx the Hesitant
- Parents unknown; a foundling of the Foundling House of Perorigak, given the house surname **Perorin**.
  No known kin. The Children of Rede claimed his "spiritual sons".

### Jhoeland Leneu, the Seagull
- **Aumery Leneu**, net-mender of the Perostro docks × **Brisse**
  - **Jhoeland** (1672/c. 1760)
  - **Liss** (1680/1745), whose life, and their mother's, Jhoeland bought with Beatilda's.

### House Avory
- **Hobb Avory**, IISTG pilot of Perostro
  - **Dosh Avory** (1695/1779), conqueror of the three Avorias, × **Claude Mervant**
    - **Tancrède Avory** (1725/1801), first Governor of the Avorias
      - ... a line of hereditary governors ...
        - **Jourdain Avory** (2470/2540), last governor, deposed by Edilon III in 2533 and sent to Perpetua.

### Gereston Bolish
- Son of a Dokur fur-trader, **Anselm Bolish**. Unmarried. His *Northern Letters* were addressed to his
  sister **Maud**.

### Cenzus Fogun
- Son of Napelese tenant farmers, **Tibalt** and **Gile Fogun**, of the levee village of Oxmere. Celibate.
  A nephew, **Aubry Fogun**, became a Genesive bishop on Ossomire.

### Arneviz Geoant
- **Ludo Geoant**, schoolmaster of Perorigak × **Sabine**
  - **Arneviz** (2790/vanished 2871)
  - **Clothilde Geoant** (2795/2880), who edited and published his *Relations of the Far Worlds*

### Dusher Polosik
- **Pell Polosik**, ferryman at Lusk × **Hawise**
  - **Dusher** (960/1060). Unmarried. His disciple and successor was **Agnel of Hurn**.

### The IISTG pioneers
- **Barnoix Barnutum** (790/860) was the grandson of **Kaeros Lougi**'s navigator, **Umbert Barnutum**, and
  named Kaeros as his teacher. Otherwise the pioneers were unrelated.

### Nemeth Oraun
- A herder of the northern Belune hills; parents unknown. The Belunese Remnant trace descent from his
  sister's children, "the Oraunish".
