## Notes for the maintainer

- `todo.txt` still carries an **older** lore sketch under `## LORE`, with different branch names (Existantialism,
  Realism, Eternalism, Pluralism, Nihilism, Refutism, Heretism) and "King of Ban" instead of Baron.
  `lore-draft.txt` and this folder supersede it. It was left untouched.
- The game's clock counts only days (`GameClock`), and lifespans are 30 to 120 rounds of 360 days
  (`LifetimeStat`). The lore's round of 360 days and its preference for day-counts in speech agree with both.
- The game's humors (blood, phlegm, yellow and black bile, the emotion humors, the consumed humors) and
  *modi mentis* are placed in the world's medicine in `03_world_science.md` §6.
- The game's world-selection sky holds 383 moons. The lore's star-tables list 383 moons for that reason.
