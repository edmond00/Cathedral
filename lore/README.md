# Lore of Proscribed Palimpsest

This folder is the **source material** for everything the game says about its world's past. It is
never shown to the player. The player meets the world's history only **indirectly**: in books,
chronicles, sermons, songs, inscriptions and hearsay, and each of those tells its own version,
partial, biased, garbled, forged or scraped off and overwritten.

These files record **what actually happened**, so that every in-game source can be written as a
deliberate distortion of it. When a book contradicts these files, the book is wrong on purpose, and
the files say who would have wanted it wrong and why (see `11_texts_and_sources.md`).

## Conventions

- **Dating.** All dates are given in **FC** (*Foundation Count*), in *rounds* counted from the day
  Aqilon I entered Dokur and the half-elf empire was born (0 FC). A round is 360 days. Negative
  numbers are before the Foundation. After the destruction of Pyr, dates are also given in **AH**
  (*After the Hatching*): 0 AH = 3579 FC. In-world people count in days, and in rounds only in
  learned writing; see `03_world_science.md` ("Reckoning time") and `10_culture_and_daily_life.md`.
- **"Years".** The draft speaks in years. Here a "year" is a round, and the words are
  interchangeable in these files. In-world sources prefer days: "four thousand days in the dark".
- **Truth labels.** Where a fact is known to nobody in the world, it is marked **[hidden]**. Where
  these files propose an answer the draft did not give and which may be vetoed without disturbing
  anything else, it is marked **[proposal]**. Everything else is established.
- **No sun.** No world in this cosmology has a sun. Daylight comes through the night shell from the
  embers of the burnt forest; see `03_world_science.md`. A source that speaks of "the sun" is using
  folk speech for the brightest part of the day-sky, the Eye of Day.
- **Names.** Every Ban is numbered in the Register of Blood. A numbered name (Naabus VII) means
  every lower number of that name exists in `06_lineages.md`.

## Tone

Antiquity, Middle Ages and the Early Modern period, seen through a dark lens: dynasties that feed on
a prisoner, religions forged for revenue, wars won by plague and fire, and a cosmos that is quietly
eating its inhabitants. Nothing here is heroic without a price, and nothing is monstrous without
reasons that made sense to someone at the time.
