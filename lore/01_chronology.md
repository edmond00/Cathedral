# Chronology

Dates in **FC** (*Foundation Count*): rounds of 360 days counted from the capitulation of Pebelos at
Dokur (0 FC). After the destruction of Pyr, also in **AH** (*After the Hatching*, 0 AH = 3579 FC).
"c." marks a date that no source fixes to the round. Durations given in days are how contemporaries
reckoned them.

Era names are the ones later historians use. Nobody living through the Vahyrian Empire called it that.

---

## 0. Before time (undated)

- The Principia come to be, each the answer to a question (Ten Chapters, 0 to 6).
- Qoth makes the Random Forest; Repance burns it (Ch. 7). The wood elves become ash elves.
- Qoth makes the Cosmic Orgy; Repance rots it (Ch. 8). The blood elves weep, sink into
  torpor and drift.
- Qoth makes the Eternal Temple; Repance wears it to sand (Ch. 9). The white elves are buried with it.
- The Cosmic Sea is ordered: ashes up, sand down, tears between (Ch. 10).
- The first giant jellyfish appear in the cosmic sea and begin to lay eggs. **[hidden]** Where the
  first jellyfish came from is told by no chapter; see `12_secrets_and_mysteries.md`.

## I. The deep past of Pyr (before c. −1600 FC)

- **Egg of Pyr laid**: age unknown. Imperial philosophers later estimated Pyr's shells at "some
  hundreds of thousands of rounds" from their layering; the number is a guess.
- Humans on the outer shell of Pyr since before memory. Oldest traces: shell-middens and stone
  fish-weirs on the Vunic coast, ring-forts on the Harrow Range.
- The dwarfs of Pyr's hollow layer are never met in historic times, but Vunic cave-lore and the
  "deep-bells" of the Harrow miners suggest they are there.
