# Peoples and Creatures

---

## Humans

Humans live on the outer shells of the eggmoons. Every inhabited world has them, and nobody knows why.
The Ten Chapters do not mention humans. Principist doctrine holds that Qoth made them as the **fourth
guests**, the inhabitants of the fourth world, mortal because the fourth world is the last. Medusosians
hold that humans are the embryo's **dreams**, grown on the shell to be gathered back in. **[hidden]**
Neither is known to be true, and these files do not decide it.

Humans of different worlds are **interfertile** and broadly alike, differing in colouring, build and custom
as peoples of one continent do.

### Peoples of Pyr
- **Benepelopites**: southern, olive-skinned, urban, mercantile, devout, the old heart of the empire.
- **Napelese**: river-folk, heavy-set, conservative, great eaters of bread; they remember the burning of Yr.
- **Penarans**: hill people, bridge-builders, lawyers and schoolmasters.
- **Pebelenes**: tall, pale, northern mountain clans; fur-trappers, miners, soldiers, rebels.
- **Vunic**: islanders of the Yellow Island; small, weathered, dark-haired; fishermen, salt-miners and
  sailors; famously silent.

### Peoples of the Nine Pearls (a selection)
- **Belunese Remnant**: survivors of Belune's native tribes, hunters and herders of the high valleys;
  Medusosian; wary of anyone from Pyr.
- **Prunilese**: mountain-city folk, salt-guildsmen and smugglers, fiercely local.
- **Canopy Folk** of Green Avoria: tree-dwellers, climbers, hunters.
- **Blue Avorians**: islanders, divers, navigators; famous singers, courtesans and pearl-traders.
- **Dune-clans** of Golden Avoria: caravaneers and oasis-keepers; now keepers of the pyramid's approaches.
- **Ice-cantors** of New Varam: glacier-dwellers whose throat-song carries for leagues.

### The imperial human
The Cosmic Empire (2530 to 3579) tried to make one people out of many: uniform military schooling,
Principist catechism, resettlement, **eugenics** (licensed marriages, sterilisation of "degenerate" lines)
and **genocide** of peoples who resisted. On many worlds the native population is gone or mixed beyond
recognition, and the villages speak a descendant of **Imperial** (the language of Avolor).

## The Ban, "half-elves"

Not a people but a family: humans altered by elf blood. See `06_lineages.md` and `03_world_science.md`.
- **Ban-Aloid**: tall (~2 m), long-lived (~200 rounds), fine-featured, with a faintly elvish cast of ear and
  eye. They believed themselves half-elves.
- **The Old House**: ~500 rounds; stopped growing, kept ageing in appearance; ancient-looking but vigorous.
- **Ban-Balnus**: 2,000+ rounds; giants; deformed; in the end sterile. Lwrash at 4 m, Rosena at 4 m and
  skeletal, Oppretitette with three-metre arms, Akilon XII at 3 m and vast, Oox at 10 m, Vesifia III at
  25 m, furred and five-armed.

After the Hatching, "half-elf" is a word for something monstrous, or grand, or both.

## Elves

The immortal people of the three destroyed worlds. See `02_cosmology.md`.

| | appearance | nature | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wood elves** | humanoid plants, skin like thin bark | chaotic growth, the forest's own life | gone; become ash elves |
| **Ash elves** | coal-skinned, cracked, glowing within | the burnt, still living | the ash-sky, among the embers |
| **Blood elves** | human-seeming, flawless, long-lobed ears, eyes without whites | appetite, beauty, and grief | the cosmic sea, unconscious |
| **White elves** | marble-skinned, cold, pale-haired | worship, order, judgment | presumed in the sand-floor |

Only one elf is known to have lived on a world in historic times: **the Guest**, the Ban's prisoner.
Folklore on many worlds speaks of "sea-saints", "drowned angels" and "the pale swimmer", and **[proposal]**
some of these may be other blood elves that fell through vortices over the ages.

**[hidden]** The Guest was on Pyr when it hatched. An elf does not die. See `12_secrets_and_mysteries.md`.

## Dwarfs

The people of the **hollow layer**, the cavern-world between the inner shells of every eggmoon.

- **Appearance**: short, broad, heavy-boned; pale grey or bluish skin; large dark eyes adapted to the
  fungus-light; very long-lived by human standards.
- **Life**: they live on fungi, blind cave-animals, and the warmth that rises from the inferior inner shell;
  they build in stone; they are organised in **kingdoms** under **bell-kings**. Belune's is **Oggerund**.
- **Faith**: they worship the **embryo** as the god-being-born and its eventual hatching as the unification
  of all souls. For them this is not faith but observation: they live against the embryo's shell and can
  feel it grow. They are the source of **Medusosianism**.
- **Contact**: extremely rare. Nemeth Oraun (Belune, c. −900); a few hermits and miners on other worlds.
  They are uninterested in the outer shell, which they call **"the crust"**, and in the people on it.
- **[proposal]** Dwarfs regard humans as the embryo's **herd**: surface beings whose deaths feed the god.
  They find human religions touching.

Surface folklore on most worlds knows dwarfs as "the deep folk", "bell-ringers under the hill", and
counts the sound of bells from deep mines as a death-omen.

## Goblins

**Unknown to every imperial archive.** First written account c. 70 AH, on **Mirelle**; within three
centuries reported on most worlds that keep records.

- **Appearance**: small (child-sized to a man's chest), wiry, **green-skinned**, **pig-nosed**, with large
  ears, sharp teeth, and clawed hands.
- **Behaviour**: in bands; nocturnal or twilight-loving; tunnel under fields and ruins; raid livestock and
  stores; take children, according to every village. They speak a **click-language**. They use stolen tools
  and make crude ones. They are unafraid, which is what people remark on most.
- **Where**: caves, old mines, ruins, and especially **imperial ruins** and the **deep places**.
- **Origin**: unknown in the world. Theories in circulation after the Hatching: the ghosts of Pyr; the
  children of the Last Empress; dwarfs gone bad; a punishment of Repance; the "elf of the temple" that came
  back to Pyr.
- The last archives of the empire describe the creature brought back by the *Patience of Rhesis* as small,
  pig-nosed and clawed, but **red**.

**[hidden] [proposal]** The origin of goblins is given in `12_secrets_and_mysteries.md`.

## The cosmic jellyfish

- **Size**: beyond measure; a bell "wider than the Inland Seas", threads "longer than a voyage".
- **Life**: they drift in the cosmic sea, rising toward the ash-sky to feed on ember-light (**[proposal]**
  and on ash elves) and sinking to lay their eggs at middle depth.
- **Eggs**: every world. See `03_world_science.md`.
- **Newborns**: a hatched world becomes a young jellyfish carrying every soul the embryo gathered. Varam's
  (1703) can be seen faintly from nearby worlds on clear nights.
- **Worship**: Medusosianism; Beatildism; many hollow-cults.

## Beasts of note

The animals of the outer shells are those of ordinary country life on every world: horses, cattle, swine,
goats, poultry, dogs, cats, wolves, bears, foxes, boars, deer, birds, insects. Some worlds have their own:
- the **giant beasts of Gorgomanth**, hunted for the imperial menageries;
- the **salt-goats** of Prunil, which lick the salt-mountains bare;
- the **tree-hogs** of Green Avoria;
- the **glass-shrimp** of Blue Avoria, source of its famous translucent dyes;
- **[proposal]** the **sea-lice** of the cosmic sea, parasites of the jellyfish big enough to hole a hull,
  feared by every submarine crew and never caught.
