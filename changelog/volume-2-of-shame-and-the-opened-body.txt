Volume 2 - Of Shame and the Opened Body
2026-08-30

Added
  - Attacking an NPC now resolves a first blow before the fight begins. The swing is
    narrated - the implement used, the wound dealt, the lesson learned. A miss still
    starts the fight and gives the enemy the first move.
  - Actions can now provoke an emotion. One of the dispositions you hold answers what
    you did, secretes humors into the spleen, and narrates its reaction in its own voice.
  - Three new humors: Constantia, which turns a 1 into a 3; Pudor, which turns a 4 into
    a 2; and Nausea, which turns a 5 into a 3.
  - Over a hundred new modi mentis, fifty-one of them dispositions that can provoke an
    emotion.
  - Butchering a carcass now yields four to eight parts. NPC corpses can be butchered
    the same way, and whatever the NPC carried is dropped beside the body rather than
    found inside it.
  - Companions can now die - of wounds, of spent humors or of age - and leave a corpse
    in the room where they fell.
  - Conversations now teach a modus mentis chosen from the dialogue branch and from who
    you spoke to, so begging a reeve teaches something different from begging a farmhand.
  - Settings now sets the size and weight of the world's glyphs separately from the
    interface's.

Changed
  - A wound can now disable a modus mentis outright, where before it only slowed its
    growth. A ruined organ drags the level ceiling below the base level; the modus
    mentis then reads BROKEN, is refused when selected, and no longer appears among
    your dialogue replies.
  - Tool use is now judged before the model is asked. No handcraft proficiency means no
    tool may be used at all; a fighting verb accepts any weapon and refuses anything
    that is not one; and an item made for one verb is refused for every other. Each
    case has its own refusal message.
  - Combining an item with a tool no longer consumes the tool.
  - Observing an object no longer excludes the object's own word from the words it
    offers, and varies which of the fitting words it gives.

Fixed
  - Losing a fight inside a location now ends the run, as losing one on the road already did.
  - An enemy killed by bleeding is now dead after the fight, instead of standing in the
    room at full health.
  - Being ambushed on the last step of a journey no longer leaves you on the world map
    with no action available.

Internal
  - Twenty-one new test scripts, and a new audit of how a player reaches each modus mentis.
