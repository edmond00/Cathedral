Volume 6 - Wherein the Will Seeks Its Nearest Road
2026-10-27

The game now plays its own music: a new soundtrack synthesised as you play, with a theme for each
world, dance tunes in taverns and markets, night music, and a heavier sound for fights. Each place
you know is remembered as a map you can open, and a way you already know can be gone back by.
Some modi mentis now want one act and plan the way to it, and Reconsider hands an act to another
modus mentis.

Added
  - A new soundtrack, synthesised by the game itself, so it sounds the same on every machine.
  - Each world has its own theme, which comes back now and then in other pieces.
  - Taverns, markets, ports and towns play jigs and reels on fiddle, tin whistle, pipes and drum.
  - Every place has night music: colder, stranger and sparser after dark.
  - Fights have their own music, a riff, drums and a lead, starting the moment the fight opens.
  - Wills: some modi mentis want one act (picking a lock, a meal, a night kept) and plan the shortest way to it. They attend to and propose only the next step on that way.
  - Reconsider, the third row of an action's popup: for one noetic point, another modus mentis takes the act over, for example a discreet one in front of a witness.
  - Mental maps: every place you know is remembered as a map, up to four places per point of anamnesis. The oldest place not locked is forgotten first, and everything known about it goes with it.
  - The location map: every move in a place is shown on its map, and Tab or the header button opens it during exploration.
  - Backtrack: a way you already know is gone back by, choosing on the map where to go.
  - Waiting now shows the hours passing and lets you choose until when. The day turns at dawn.
  - Waiting into the night in somebody's house brings the householder. Talk them round and you may stay; fail and you are shown the door.
  - Every failed act now costs something: a wound, losing your way, an hour wasted, or a noetic point.
  - A branch or a wooden stick can be used as a club in a fight. It opens the blunt fighting skills but adds no dice.
  - A menu button at the top right of every phase, and each management tab opens with its first letter from anywhere but a fight.
  - The fight panel shows each fighter's species.
  - The memory panel shows the act a will wants.

Changed
  - Craftsmen sell their own craft's goods: the cooper casks, the baker bread, the barber razors. Four new items: keg, wooden bucket, wash tub and plucked fowl.
  - A human's senses can only be turned on something by a modus mentis that observes.
  - A refused idea is taken up again at once by another modus mentis able to do it, or given up.
  - Private ground is now always behind a locked door; no open way or stair leads into it from outside.
  - Routines: choose one by its icon or with the stepper, then confirm. Arriving by a routine shows the walk to its area.
  - The Maps tab is now Chorography, and shows each place's map with its routines.
  - The waiting and routine boxes have a new look.
  - The music changes faster when you change place, and no longer stops on every outcome.

Fixed
  - Narration no longer speaks as the person being observed ("I am Jeffrey...").
  - Several modi mentis no longer invent hearths, beds or tides that are not there.
  - Explaining yourself to the person who caught you is no longer refused as "caught red-handed".
  - Closing the pause menu gives the music back.
  - Fights started on the road now play their full music instead of a single pad.
  - Dying during narration shows the death screen over the world instead of a black screen.
  - Answering NO to a doubt no longer drops the next idea from the narration.

Internal
  - Tools to measure the music, and a screenshot command for testing.
